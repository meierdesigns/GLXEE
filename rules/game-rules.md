## 🌌 MISSION OVERVIEW

*In the year 2187, the Great Galactic War has reached a critical turning point. The discovery of Voltex Crystals on the distant world of Zephyria has ignited the most devastating conflict in galactic history, pitting the United Galactic Defense Force against the relentless Nexus Collective. As the war rages across five planetary theaters, elite pilots are called upon to master the most advanced combat technology ever conceived.*

**GLXEE** represents the pinnacle of intergalactic combat simulation technology, developed by the United Galactic Defense Force (UGDF) to train elite pilots in high-stakes space warfare scenarios. This advanced tactical warfare system simulates real-world combat conditions across multiple planetary theaters, utilizing cutting-edge spacecraft and weaponry systems powered by the revolutionary Voltex Crystal technology that has become the centerpiece of the ongoing galactic conflict.

### 🎯 STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

*The success of the Great Galactic War depends on the ability of UGDF pilots to master advanced combat tactics and secure critical Voltex Crystal deposits across multiple planetary theaters.*

- **ELIMINATE NEXUS FORCES**: Engage and destroy Nexus Collective spacecraft across various planetary environments
- **SECURE VOLTEX DEPOSITS**: Navigate through treacherous asteroid fields and energy barriers to protect crystal mining operations
- **MASTER CRYSTAL WEAPONRY**: Utilize Voltex-powered weapon systems including plasma cannons, spread-fire arrays, and rapid-fire modules
- **LIBERATE PLANETARY THEATERS**: Progress through increasingly challenging planetary environments to reclaim lost territories

## 🛸 PILOT CONTROL SYSTEMS

### 🕹️ FLIGHT CONTROL INTERFACE

#### **Primary Navigation Controls**
- **Arrow Keys / WASD**: Thrust Vector Control System
  - **Left/Right**: Horizontal thrust vectoring
  - **Up/Down**: Vertical thrust vectoring (limited to tactical zone)
- **Spacebar**: Primary Weapons Fire
- **Q/E Keys**: Weapon Module Selection
- **Escape**: Emergency Tactical Pause
- **+/- Keys**: Display Resolution Scaling (classified)

#### **Tactical Zone Restrictions**
- **PLAYER ZONE**: Bottom 1/3 of tactical display (y: 200-300)
- **ENEMY ZONE**: Top 1/3 of tactical display (y: 0-200)
- **NEUTRAL ZONE**: Middle 1/3 - obstacle transit corridor
